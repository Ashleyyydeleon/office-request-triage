# Office hours, HR requests and general questions

Requests that do not belong to a specific workplace team are logged under Other and
routed to the right owner.

- Office hours: the office is open 7am to 8pm on weekdays. It is closed on company
  holidays, which are listed in the employee handbook and the shared calendar.
  Questions about hours are low.
- Employee handbook and policies: available on the intranet under "People". Questions
  about where to find them are low.
- HR letters (employment verification, visa letters) go to the People team and are
  sent within two business days (medium).
- Company events and all-hands: dates are on the shared calendar. Questions are low.
- Parking permits for employees are managed by the People team (low).
- Lost and found: items are kept at the front desk for 30 days. A lost personal item
  is medium; a lost company laptop or badge follows the IT and security policy.
