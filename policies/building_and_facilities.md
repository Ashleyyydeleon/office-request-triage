# Building and facilities

The facilities team owns the building itself: heating and cooling, lighting, power,
plumbing, restrooms, elevators, doors, locks, badge readers, signage and life-safety
equipment (alarms, exit signs, first aid kits, AEDs).

- Emergencies (leaks, power outages, elevator entrapment, doors that trap people or
  block entry) follow the emergency procedure and are high.
- Broken systems with a workaround are medium: a floor that is too hot or too cold, a
  badge reader on one door that does not work, a broken lock, a failed exit-sign
  light (fixed within one business day for safety compliance).
- Small fixes and restocks are low: a flickering light, restroom supplies, minor
  cosmetic damage.
- Fire drills and alarm tests are announced by email in advance. Questions about them
  are low.
- First aid kits are in every kitchen and next to every stairwell; AEDs are by the
  elevators on each floor. Questions about their location are low.
