# Mailroom and deliveries

The mailroom team owns packages, mail, couriers, outgoing shipments and returns.
Mailroom hours are posted on the workplace intranet page and at the mailroom door.

- Missing packages: if tracking says delivered but the package is not in the mailroom,
  the team checks the log and loading dock within one business day (medium).
- Couriers and signatures: the mailroom signs for business couriers during open hours.
  Scheduled same-day pickups or deliveries are medium.
- Time-sensitive items: perishable or temperature-controlled items (for example
  refrigerated medical or lab shipments) must be handled the same day (high).
- Damaged or leaking packages: do not open or touch; if the contents could be
  hazardous, follow the emergency procedure (high).
- Former employees: packages for people who have left are returned to the sender
  (low).
- Personal returns: employees may drop off prepaid personal returns at the mailroom
  (low). Personal deliveries should go to a home address.
