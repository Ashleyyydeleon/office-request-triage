# Visitors and front desk

The front desk team owns guest registration, check-in, visitor badges, the visitor
kiosks and visitor forms (including NDAs signed at check-in).

- Pre-registration: hosts register guests in the visitor system at least one business
  day ahead. Recurring guests can be added to the approved guest list. These are low
  urgency.
- Arrival: the system notifies the host when a guest checks in. If a host was not
  notified and a guest is waiting, the front desk handles it right away (high).
- Kiosks and badge printers: if check-in is down while guests are waiting, it is high
  urgency because guests are blocked. If a form or screen has a problem but guests
  can still check in another way, it is medium.
- Visitor parking: guests may use visitor spaces in the garage when registered in
  advance; questions about this are low urgency.
- Guests must be escorted beyond the lobby. Report anyone without a badge or escort
  to security.
