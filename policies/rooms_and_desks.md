# Meeting rooms and desks

The workplace team owns meeting room and desk bookings, room equipment (screens,
projectors, video conferencing, whiteboards) and desk equipment (monitors, docks,
chairs).

- Booking: rooms and desks are booked in the workplace app up to four weeks ahead.
  How-to questions are low urgency.
- Booking conflicts: if someone is using a room you booked and your meeting is
  starting now, the team steps in right away (high). A booking that went missing for
  a future meeting is medium: rebook as soon as possible.
- Room equipment: if equipment fails for a meeting that is starting now, it is high
  and a technician is sent. Otherwise, broken room equipment is medium.
- Desk equipment: a reserved desk with missing or broken equipment is medium; the
  team swaps the desk or replaces the equipment within a day.
- New furniture or equipment for a room, and team seating changes for a future date,
  are low urgency and go through the workplace team's monthly planning.
