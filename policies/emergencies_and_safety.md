# Emergencies and safety procedures

All of these are HIGH urgency. Safety comes before routing: call 911 and the
security desk first, then file a request so the workplace team can follow up.

- Fire, smoke or a burning smell: pull the nearest alarm, leave by the stairs, do not
  use elevators. Small appliance fires are still emergencies.
- Gas smell: leave the area, do not switch lights or devices on or off, call 911.
- Flooding or water coming through ceilings or floors: keep people away from the
  water and electrical outlets; Facilities shuts off water at the source.
- Medical emergency (someone collapsed, unresponsive, injured or fell): call 911,
  send someone to meet responders at the entrance, bring the nearest AED.
- Person trapped (elevator, locked room or lobby): call security; do not try to force
  doors or elevator hatches.
- Security threat (unknown person without a badge, theft in progress, threats of
  violence): do not confront them; call security with a description and location.
- Hazardous material (a leaking or unknown chemical, a damaged battery): keep people
  away, do not touch it, call security.

Not emergencies: scheduled fire drills, announced alarm tests, and questions about
safety equipment. These are low urgency.

Routing after the emergency call: medical emergencies, injuries and security
threats involving people go to the Safety & Security team. Building hazards (fire,
gas, leaks, flooding, chemicals) go to the team that owns the space, for example
Facilities, Kitchen or the mailroom.
