# Kitchen and pantry

The kitchen team owns food, drinks and snacks, kitchen appliances (coffee machines,
microwaves, toasters, kettles, fridges, dishwashers) and kitchen cleanliness,
including problems with the kitchen sink.

- Restocks: drinks, milk alternatives and snacks are restocked daily; requests for
  something that ran out are low, even if the message says "urgent".
- Snack and catering requests for events need three business days' notice (low).
  Allergy-friendly options are available on request.
- Broken appliances are low when other kitchens or appliances can be used, and are
  repaired within three business days. Extra appliances for a floor are low.
- Clogged or overflowing sinks are medium; if water is spreading onto the floor,
  treat it as a leak (high).
- Any appliance that sparks, smokes or catches fire is an emergency (high): unplug it
  only if safe and follow the emergency procedure.
- Fridges are cleared every Friday at 3pm. Smells or old food are low.
